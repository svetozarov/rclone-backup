rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a private, single-user application. Its owner uses it to store encrypted backups of their own files in their own Google Drive with the open-source tools rclone and Kopia.

Privacy policy

The application is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • It requests the drive.file permission, so it can access only the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent to any third party. Nothing is stored on any server other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

This is a personal tool, not a public service. It is not offered to other users.
